People at AAAMT

Hear from the people building AAAMT

Discover what our colleagues work on, what they have learned and what taking ownership means in their roles.

Challa Sharath Reddy Product

We decide which customer problems deserve attention and help teams understand what to build, why it matters and what success would look like. We encourage direct customer learning, clear priorities and open discussion of trade-offs with design, engineering and customer-facing teams. Joining us means contributing from discovery through release, developing your product judgement and taking responsibility for checking whether what we shipped actually helped.

Allamaneni Sindhuja Engineering

We turn customer problems into software that people can rely on, taking responsibility for how it is built, maintained and improved. We value curiosity, independent thinking and accountability, encouraging everyone to challenge assumptions, learn from mistakes and continuously improve how we work. Joining us means working on real product challenges and developing your technical judgement, while owning the quality of your work and learning from what happens after release.

Kiran Kumar Revenue

We help customers understand whether our products fit their needs and turn that understanding into lasting business relationships. We encourage thoughtful questions, honest commitments and shared learning from customer conversations, including opportunities we do not win. Joining us means developing your judgement across discovery, GTM and customer relationships, while taking responsibility for disciplined follow-through, accurate expectations and the business results of your work.

Sreeja Samanthapuri HR

We shape how people join, settle into their roles and experience working at AAAMT, while helping teams build clear and fair people practices. We encourage listening carefully, explaining decisions and following through on feedback, with respect for confidentiality and individual circumstances. Joining us means contributing to hiring, onboarding and employee experience, while learning to balance employee needs with business priorities and taking responsibility for the care and consistency of your work.
